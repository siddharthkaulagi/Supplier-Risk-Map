    How the score works

    A transparent, rule-based 0–100 exposure score.

    Each supplier receives points for spend concentration, country exposure, alternative availability, and onboarding lead time. If all suppliers are in one country, each supplier gets an extra dependency surcharge.

    Low · 0–30 Moderate · 31–60 High · 61–100

    A portfolio score is the spend-weighted mean of supplier scores. Risk bands for the charts and supplier list use each individual supplier's score. Scenario severity adds duration and onboarding-gap pressure to the score.
